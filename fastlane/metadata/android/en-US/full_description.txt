TunaReceive is the Android and Android TV client for TunaStream.

It installs next to Moonlight and next to Artemis. Pair it with your own TunaStream PC. The first target is an NVIDIA Shield TV Pro.

Based on Artemis by ClassicOldSong, which is based on Moonlight. GPL-3.0. This app is not Moonlight and it is not Artemis.

Sideload the arm64 APK from the GitHub releases of bigtunafish9943/TunaReceive.
